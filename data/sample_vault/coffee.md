# Brewing Pour-Over Coffee

Pour-over is a manual brewing method. Heat water to about 96°C and
pour it slowly over medium-ground beans in a circular motion.

## Grind size

A medium grind, similar to table salt, works best for a V60 dripper.
Too fine and the water drains slowly, over-extracting bitter flavors.
